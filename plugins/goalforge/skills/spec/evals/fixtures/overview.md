## Problem

Services need a reliable way to deliver notifications via email and SMS when events occur.

## Goal

Users receive notifications within 5 seconds of a triggering event.

## Scope

**In**: Email delivery, SMS delivery, event subscription API.
**Out**: Push notifications, in-app notifications.
