## Idea

Fixture: a terminal idea already archived to `ideas/_archived/`. Its slug is
ABSENT from the live `ideas/` root, so a slug-availability check that only looks
at live paths reads it as free.

## Why it matters

Second half of the goalforge-capture Step 2 archived-collision probe: the idea
track archives to `ideas/_archived/<slug>.md`, and a promoted idea's slug is
normally the feature slug. Capture must probe both archived locations before
treating a slug as absent.
